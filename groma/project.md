description: Self-hosted distributed runtime for Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects.
---

celld runs a Wrangler application on machines you own, with S3-compatible storage for long-term state. A node serves Workers and Durable Objects; the nodes that share one bucket form a fleet. This repository is the runtime, not a product application or the 1us platform.
